## 📝 The Example Prompt Used to Generate This Website

You can give this exact prompt (or modify it for your own topic) to any AI assistant (such as **Gemini Pro** in **Google Antigravity**, or Claude):

```text
Create a cinematic scrollytelling website for my audiophile hardware startup called 'KINETIX' and our flagship in-ear monitor 'AETHER ONE'.

Key requirements:
1. Pinned 3D Stage: Begin with the earphone fully assembled and suspended along an engineered 38.4° diagonal 3D vector.
2. 3D Diagonal Exploded Breakdown: As the user scrolls, rotate the earphone in 3D and explode its 7 internal electronic, power, and acoustic transducer layers along the 38.4° diagonal axis. Show leader-line callout pins and an active telemetry HUD.
3. 7 Sub-Assemblies: Include (1) silicone isolation tip, (2) CNC Grade-5 titanium nozzle, (3) coaxial dual-BA tweeter, (4) 11.4mm pure Beryllium dynamic driver with copper coil and N52 magnet, (5) 4-layer ENIG gold flex PCB with KX-DSP2 neural chip, (6) 65mAh curved solid-state Li-Si battery, and (7) Grade-5 titanium unibody shell with smoked sapphire touch faceplate.
4. Magnetic Re-Merge: As the user scrolls through the breakdown, magnetically re-converge all 7 modules back into the unified chassis with an acoustic lock shockwave.
5. Deep Business & Technical Sections: Follow with a scroll-driven dual marquee, letter-by-letter manifesto, a clinical cleanroom inversion seam (#F3F3EE), an interactive 7-layer BOM explorer, a real-time neural ANC simulation lab with live waveform canvas, sticky stacking product/business cards, and an interactive finish configurator (Obsidian PVD, Raw Titanium, Founders 18K Gold).
6. Format: Output as a single, self-contained HTML file that runs directly in any modern browser without npm dependencies.
```

## 🔄 Adapting the Prompt for ANY Physical Product or Machine

You can adapt this prompt for any topic where internal craftsmanship is the hero. Simply swap the subject and components:

### Example 1: Mechanical Chronograph Watch
> *"Create a 3D scrollytelling launch page for a bespoke Swiss mechanical chronograph watch. As you scroll, tilt the watch along a 3D diagonal angle and explode the 6 internal movement layers: sapphire crystal, ceramic tachymeter bezel, dial with blued steel hands, column-wheel chronograph module, ETA automatic escapement with perlage finish, and open sapphire caseback with 22K gold oscillating rotor. Then magnetically re-merge the watch as you scroll, followed by technical caliber specs and reservation modal."*

### Example 2: Electric Vehicle Drone / Robotics
> *"Create a 3D scrollytelling website for an autonomous carbon-fiber surveying drone. On scroll, execute an axial 3D exploded breakdown showing: aerodynamic prop array, brushless gimbal motor, 4K LiDAR optical sensor payload, dual-redundant flight controller PCB, swappable solid-state battery pack, and monocoque carbon-fiber unibody frame. Re-merge the drone on further scroll with aerodynamic telemetry HUD."*

### Example 3: Cinema Camera / Optical Lens
> *"Create a scrollytelling website for an anamorphic cinema lens. On scroll, separate the 9 optical glass elements along the optical axis, highlighting fluorite coatings, 14-blade aperture iris, and titanium PL mount, then re-merge into the sealed cinema barrel."*
