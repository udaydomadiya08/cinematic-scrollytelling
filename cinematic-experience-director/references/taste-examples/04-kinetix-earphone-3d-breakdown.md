# KINETIX // AETHER ONE — 3D Acoustic & Electronic Scrollytelling Showcase

Build a motion-rich, browser-verified scrollytelling flagship launch experience for an audiophile hardware startup called **KINETIX** and their flagship in-ear monitor **AETHER // ONE**. The experience features an intricate, interactive 3D diagonal exploded-view breakdown of 7 internal electronic, power, and acoustic transducer layers that separate along an engineered 38.4° diagonal vector as the user scrolls, orbit 360° in 3D space with interactive callout pins and an electronic BOM inspector, and then **magnetically re-merge back into the unified titanium chassis** with an acoustic lock shockwave.

## 5-Act Narrative Architecture

### Act I: Pinned 580vh 3D Diagonal Scrollytelling Stage
A sticky 100vh viewport pinned over a 580vh scroll track:
1. **State 1 (0.00 – 0.13):** Assembled & unified at a 38.4° diagonal acoustic vector with ambient radial lighting.
2. **State 2 (0.13 – 0.42):** 3D diagonal rotation and continuous separation of 7 internal hardware modules along the 38.4° axis.
3. **State 3 (0.42 – 0.65):** Full 100% 3D explosion. Camera orbits around the internal stack; interactive leader-line callout pins appear; right HUD inspects active micro-electronics.
4. **State 4 (0.65 – 0.87):** Magnetic re-convergence. Reverse diagonal vector pulls all 7 modules back toward the center.
5. **State 5 (0.87 – 1.00):** Hermetic acoustic lock. The parts snap into ±2.0μm alignment triggering a dual-color cyan/copper shockwave pulse.

#### The 7 Exploded Hardware Sub-Assemblies:
- **Layer 01:** Dual-Density Medical Silicone Isolation Tip (Shore 35A/50A, -26dB seal).
- **Layer 02:** CNC Grade-5 Titanium Nozzle & 18μm Micro-Pore Damper Mesh.
- **Layer 03:** Coaxial Dual-Balanced Armature Ultra-High Tweeter Array (12kHz–48kHz).
- **Layer 04:** 11.4mm Pure Beryllium Diaphragm, 240-Turn Copper Coil & 1.6T N52 Magnet.
- **Layer 05:** 4-Layer ENIG Gold Flex PCB & 22nm QFN-48 KX-DSP2 Neural ANC Silicon Core.

- **Layer 07:** Bead-Blasted Grade-5 Titanium Unibody Shell, Copper Bass Vent & 9H Smoked Sapphire Touch Faceplate.

#### Dual Rendering Engine:
